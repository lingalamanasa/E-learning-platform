Built by Engineers, Refined by Science

Founded in 2022 by senior distributed systems architects and machine learning researchers, STACKLY started with a single conviction: traditional education produces passive consumers of theory, while modern engineering demands intuitive, hands-on muscle memory.

Today, over 120,000 learners across 84 countries use STACKLY to simulate real-world cloud architectures, fine-tune neural models in browser sandboxes, and secure verified credentials evaluated by automated telemetry.
